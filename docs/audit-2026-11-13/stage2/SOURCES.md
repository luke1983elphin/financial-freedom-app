## Return conventions are not tax law

The following are product choices/assumptions, not verified government prescriptions: 7% investment return, 6.5% super, the automatic 1-point retired-super reduction, zero default fee, APR versus effective annual meaning, midpoint cash movements, no sale/CGT strategy, annual pension CPI proxy, future unindexed tax brackets and household access ages. A fund may publish returns already net of investment tax and some costs; the app does not establish whether these defaults were taken from such a source.

A qualified current-policy review must also cover contribution/work/age tests, carry-forward eligibility and balances, transfer caps, pension minimums, exempt-current-pension-income conditions, untaxed funds, franking eligibility, Medicare reductions, and any high-balance super earnings-tax regime applicable at the chosen projection date. This review does not certify unsupported high-balance/defined-benefit/specialist cases. Do not model their policy simply by adding an assumed rate to all returns.

## How to read the confidence labels

The code evidence for a missing tax payment, duplicate property or ignored credit is directly reproducible. The actual individual's tax liability requires the stated residency, ownership, cover, income character and eligibility assumptions. Current pension parameter checks increase confidence in the dated ordinary schedule, but cannot establish a future pension entitlement. Unavailable source content is recorded as unavailable, never converted into a current-rule claim by inference from the code.
